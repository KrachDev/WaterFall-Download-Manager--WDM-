Waterfall Companion Extension Setup Guide

Quick 30-Second Browser Integration for High-Speed Multi-Stream Capture

📁 Extension Location on Your Computer
Waterfall includes the ready-to-load browser companion extension inside your installation directory. Click below to open the extension folder directly in Windows Explorer:
Unpacked Extension Directory Path: Manifest V3
1
Open Your Browser's Extensions Page
Open a new tab in Google Chrome and navigate to chrome://extensions, or click the Extensions puzzle icon in the toolbar and select Manage Extensions.
chrome://extensions
2
Enable "Developer Mode"
Look at the top-right corner of the Extensions page and toggle the Developer mode switch to ON. This unlocks the ability to load unpacked extensions.
Top-Right Corner:
Developer mode
3
Click "Load Unpacked"
A new sub-toolbar will appear on the top-left of the Extensions page. Click the button labeled Load unpacked.
Top-Left Toolbar:
Load unpacked
4
Select the Waterfall "extension" Folder
In the folder selection dialog, navigate to the folder you opened in Step 1 and select the extension folder, then click Select Folder.
Selected Folder: 📁 ...\Waterfall\extension
5
Pin Waterfall & Enjoy High-Speed Downloads
Click the puzzle icon 🧩 in your browser toolbar, find Waterfall Download Accelerator, and click the Pin 📌 icon. Now whenever you click a download link in your browser, Waterfall will automatically intercept it with multi-stream acceleration!
